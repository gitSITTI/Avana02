What the V is, and what this app does to it

The V is a way of laying out systems engineering. Down the left you go from what the stakeholders need to system requirements, then subsystem and component requirements. At the bottom the thing is designed and built. Up the right you show each level is met, from components to acceptance by the people who asked for it.

Every level on the left has a matching check on the right, and that pairing is what the dashed lines show. Requirements are verified by test, analysis, inspection or demonstration, and each verification item points at the requirement it proves.

Consolidation removes requirements from the left. If a removed requirement had verification items, those items must be pointed at the one that survives, or that level is left with checks that prove nothing. The “to carry over” number counts them, and the trace map in Export lists each one.

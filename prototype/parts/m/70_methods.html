Methods and metrics

How well each way of working finds the right pairs, and what the AI adds when it comes after the mechanical steps or before them. Every source is scored the same way, so the comparison is fair.

Only the first table is measured. It comes from pairs people have labelled (see Label pairs) and shows a range. Every score below it, including the four ways of working and the AI before and after, is still a placeholder that shows the layout, until the engine scores pairs and the labels cover them. The “This session” blocks are counted from your own decisions.

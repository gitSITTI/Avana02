New here? Three things to do on each cluster
A cluster is a small group of requirements that look alike. You decide what happens to each one.
  1. Read the wordings side by side. Coloured words are numbers, units, shall/should and not.
  2. Check the guards. A red guard means the suggestion changes something that matters.
  3. Decide in the Resolve panel, then the lenses the policy requires sign off before anything is approved.

Sign-off policy

Four lenses exist, but asking all four to sign every cluster of a 1,000-requirement project is what makes reviews stall. The policy asks for the lenses that matter for each case, and samples the rest on a fixed 20% so the same clusters are always chosen.
